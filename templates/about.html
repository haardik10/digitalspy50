About DigitalSpy

DigitalSpy is built to tackle misinformation using intelligent news analysis, source tracking, and AI-driven verification workflows. Our goal is to help users assess content faster, reduce noise, and make better judgments about the credibility of what they read online.

We combine machine learning, article parsing, feed aggregation, and frontend usability into one focused system designed for practical verification and fast decision-making.
